# Hi, I'm Kirron 👋

## IT Support | Networking | Windows | Linux

I started my technical career in the U.S. Army working in Signal Support, with much of my hands-on experience centered around retransmission radio operations and communications systems. My role required me to configure equipment, troubleshoot connectivity and signal issues, maintain reliable communications, and solve technical problems in operational environments.

After transitioning out of the military, I decided to build on that communications background and move deeper into information technology. Networking felt like a natural progression because many of the same fundamentals still apply: devices need to communicate, connections need to be maintained, and problems have to be isolated and corrected.

Today, I am developing practical experience through hands-on labs focused on networking, Windows administration, Linux, Azure, troubleshooting, and IT support. I document my projects in GitHub with an emphasis on understanding how systems work, identifying why problems occur, applying the correct fix, and verifying the solution.

I am looking for an opportunity in IT support, networking, or junior systems administration where I can apply my military communications experience while continuing to develop my networking, Windows, and Linux skills.
